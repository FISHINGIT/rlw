Rod Licence Waivers, 2026-2027: Overview

0
Rod Licence Waivers issued
A rod licence waiver lets people fish at an approved Angling Trust event without first buying an Environment Agency rod licence, removing the first barrier to a first cast. Waivers are issued directly by Angling Trust Regional Officers, and to organisers listing events on the Angling Trust Event Marketplace.
